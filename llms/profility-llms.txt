# Profility

> Profility, Inc. is a Boston-based healthcare technology company whose AI-powered
> platform supports collaboration and decision-making across the post-acute care
> continuum. Profility ships its platform as an end-user web application and does
> not publish a public API, developer portal, or machine-readable specification.

This file is generated by API Evangelist from the public Profility surface. It is an
independent third-party profile, not a Profility publication.

## What Profility does

- Personalized care-planning profiles built by matching a patient against large
  historical datasets of comparable patients.
- Predictive decision support for post-acute placement: likelihood of successful
  rehabilitation and readmission risk across post-acute settings.
- PReP Authorize: automation of prior authorization and concurrent review for
  managed-care patients in skilled nursing and home health, including managed-care
  contract intelligence, denial reduction, and audit preparedness.
- Analytical reporting that scores post-acute providers and maps local market
  position, used for referral-pattern optimization and strategic positioning.
- Serves skilled nursing facilities, home health, behavioral health, dialysis
  providers, hospitals, and payers. Reported deployment across 500+ facilities.
